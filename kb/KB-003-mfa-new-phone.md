---
id: KB-003
title: Set up multi-factor authentication on a new phone
queue: identity-access
tags: [mfa, authenticator, new phone, verification, two-factor]
---
If you have a new phone, move your multi-factor authentication (MFA) to it before you wipe or hand back the old one.

1. Install Microsoft Authenticator on the new phone.
2. On a computer, go to https://aka.ms/mysecurityinfo and sign in.
3. Choose Add sign-in method, then Authenticator app, and scan the QR code with the new phone.
4. Approve the test notification, then remove the old phone from the list.

If you no longer have the old phone and cannot sign in, contact the service desk. A technician will confirm your identity with your manager and then reset your MFA registration so you can set it up again. Allow up to one business day for this.
