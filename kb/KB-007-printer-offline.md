id: KB-007
title: Office printer shows as offline
queue: service-desk
tags: [printer, offline, print, queue, toner, paper]
---
When a printer shows as offline, check whether the problem is the printer or your laptop.

1. Look at the printer screen for paper jams, low toner or an error message.
2. Ask a colleague to print a test page. If nobody can print, turn the printer off for 30 seconds and back on.
3. If only you cannot print, make sure your laptop is on the office network, not a hotspot or guest Wi-Fi.
4. Open Printers and scanners in Settings, remove the printer, then add it again.

If nobody in the office can print after a restart, contact the service desk and tell us the printer model and any error on its screen. If only one person is affected, a technician can reinstall the printer on that laptop remotely.
