queue: procurement

To buy new equipment or software, ask the service desk for a quote. Please tell us:
- what you need and how many
- who will use it and by when
- any budget limit or preferred brand

We reply with a quote, usually within two business days. Once someone with purchasing approval accepts the quote, we order the item and set it up before delivery. Standard laptops usually arrive within five to ten business days, depending on stock.

For licence renewals, we contact you before the renewal date. If you want to reduce or add licences, tell us at least two weeks before the renewal so the change can be made in time.
