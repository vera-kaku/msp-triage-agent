title: Mailbox is full or close to its storage limit
queue: service-desk
tags: [mailbox full, storage, quota, email, archive, size]
---
When your mailbox is nearly full, Outlook warns you and may stop sending email.

To free up space:
1. Empty the Deleted Items and Junk folders.
2. Search for large emails: in Outlook, sort by size, and delete or save large attachments to OneDrive.
3. Check the Sent Items folder, which often holds old attachments.

If your role needs to keep a lot of email, ask the service desk about enabling an online archive. The archive moves older email to separate storage that you can still search in Outlook. We will check whether your Microsoft 365 plan includes it before turning it on. It can take about a day to start moving email.
