---
id: KB-006
title: OneDrive is not syncing or shows a red cross
queue: service-desk
tags: [onedrive, sync, red cross, files, cloud icon]
---
A red cross or a paused icon on OneDrive means some files have not uploaded yet.

1. Click the OneDrive cloud icon near the clock and read the message at the top.
2. If it says sync is paused, choose Resume syncing.
3. If it lists a file that cannot sync, check that the file is closed in all apps and that its name does not contain characters such as # or %.
4. If it asks you to sign in, sign in with your work account.
5. Restart the laptop if the icon is still red.

Files that have not synced exist only on your laptop. Do not delete them or reset the laptop until they show a green tick. If the problem continues, contact the service desk and include a screenshot of the OneDrive message.
