id: KB-009
title: Office Wi-Fi or internet is slow or dropping out
queue: network
tags: [wifi, wi-fi, internet, slow, dropping, connection, network]
---
Slow or unreliable Wi-Fi can come from your device, the access point or the internet line.

Please check:
- Whether it affects only you or the whole office. Ask two colleagues.
- Whether a cabled computer is also slow. If cabled devices are fine, the issue is Wi-Fi.
- Where you are sitting. Meeting rooms far from an access point often have weak signal.

If only your device is affected, turn Wi-Fi off and on, then restart the device. If the whole office is slow or offline, contact the service desk with the time it started. We monitor the office line and access points, and can check with the internet provider if there is an outage in your area.
