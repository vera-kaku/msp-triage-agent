title: Reset a forgotten Microsoft 365 password
queue: identity-access

If you have forgotten your Microsoft 365 password, you can usually reset it yourself.

1. Go to https://aka.ms/sspr from any browser, including your phone.
2. Enter your work email address and complete the check.
3. Verify with the method you registered, such as the Authenticator app or a text message.
4. Choose a new password that you have not used before.

After the reset, sign in again on your laptop, phone and Outlook. Outlook and Teams may ask for the new password once.

If self-service reset does not work because you never registered a verification method, contact the service desk. A technician will confirm your identity with your manager before resetting the password. We never ask for your current password.
