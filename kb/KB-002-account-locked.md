---
id: KB-002
title: Account locked after too many sign-in attempts
queue: identity-access
tags: [locked, lockout, sign-in, attempts, account]
---
Your account locks for a short time after several wrong passwords in a row. This protects it from people guessing your password.

What to do:
- Wait 15 minutes and try again with the correct password.
- Check that Caps Lock is off and that your keyboard language has not changed.
- If you recently changed your password, update it on your phone and any other device. Old passwords saved on a phone are a common cause of repeated lockouts.

If the account locks again straight away, or you did not make the failed attempts yourself, tell the service desk. Lockouts you did not cause can be a sign that someone is trying to get into your account, and a technician will check the sign-in logs.
