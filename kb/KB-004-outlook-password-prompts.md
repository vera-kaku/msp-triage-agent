title: Outlook keeps asking for a password or stops syncing
queue: service-desk
tags: [outlook, password prompt, not syncing, disconnected, email]
---
Repeated password prompts or a "Disconnected" status in Outlook are usually caused by an old saved credential.

Try these steps in order:
1. Close Outlook completely, then open it again.
2. Check that you can sign in to https://outlook.office.com in a browser. If you cannot, the problem is your account, not Outlook. See KB-001 or KB-002.
3. Sign out of Office: in any Office app, go to File, Account, Sign out, then sign back in.
4. Restart the laptop.

If email works in the browser but Outlook still does not sync after these steps, contact the service desk with a screenshot of the message. A technician can rebuild your Outlook profile remotely. This takes about 20 minutes and you will not lose any email.
