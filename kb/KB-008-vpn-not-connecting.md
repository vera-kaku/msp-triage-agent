title: VPN will not connect when working from home
queue: network
tags: [vpn, forticlient, remote, home, connect, tunnel]
---
If the VPN client will not connect from home, check your own internet connection first.

1. Open a website such as a news site. If it does not load, the problem is your home internet or Wi-Fi.
2. Restart the VPN client and try again.
3. Check that you are using your current password. A recent password change can stop the VPN until you sign in to the laptop once in the office or update the saved password in the client.
4. If you are on a hotel or public network, try a phone hotspot instead. Some public networks block VPN traffic.

If several people cannot connect at the same time, the VPN gateway itself may be down. Tell the service desk straight away and mention how many people are affected, because this is treated as urgent.
