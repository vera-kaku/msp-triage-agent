---
id: KB-010
title: Report a suspicious email
queue: security
tags: [phishing, suspicious email, scam, report, spam, link, attachment]
---
If an email looks suspicious, do not click links, open attachments or reply.

Signs of a phishing email include an unexpected invoice or shared file, a sender address that does not match the company name, urgent requests to pay or change bank details, and requests to sign in to view a document.

To report it:
1. Use the Report button in Outlook and choose Phishing.
2. Delete the email.
3. If the email asks to change a supplier's bank account, call the supplier on a number you already have, not one in the email.

If you have already clicked a link, opened an attachment or entered your password, follow KB-011 straight away.
