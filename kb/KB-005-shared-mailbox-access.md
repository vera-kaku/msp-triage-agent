title: Request access to a shared mailbox or folder
queue: identity-access
tags: [shared mailbox, access, permission, folder, sharepoint, approval]
---
Access to shared mailboxes, SharePoint sites and shared folders needs approval from the owner or your manager.

To request access, send the service desk:
- the name of the mailbox, site or folder
- whether you need to read only, or also send and edit
- who approved the request (forward their email or copy them in)

Requests with approval are usually completed within one business day. A shared mailbox appears in Outlook on its own within about an hour of access being granted. If it does not appear, restart Outlook.

We cannot grant access without approval, even for urgent requests, because shared mailboxes often hold customer or financial information.
