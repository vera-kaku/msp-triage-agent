---
id: KB-015
title: Offboard a staff member who is leaving
queue: identity-access
tags: [leaver, leaving, offboarding, resignation, disable account, mailbox]
---
Tell us as soon as you know someone is leaving, with their last day and the time their access should stop.

On the agreed time we:
- block sign-in and sign the person out of all devices
- convert their mailbox to a shared mailbox and give access to their manager if requested
- set an automatic reply if requested
- move their OneDrive files to the manager or a nominated person
- remove licences so you are no longer billed for them

If the departure is not on good terms, call the service desk so that access can be removed straight away. Please arrange for the laptop and phone to be returned before the last day so we can wipe and reuse them.
