---
id: KB-012
title: Unexpected sign-in alert or MFA request you did not make
queue: security
tags: [suspicious sign-in, mfa request, alert, unusual location, impossible travel, approve]
---
If you get an MFA request you did not start, or an alert about a sign-in from an unusual place, someone may have your password.

- Deny the MFA request. Never approve a request you did not start, even if the prompts keep coming.
- Do not reply to the alert email or click its links.
- Contact the service desk straight away.

A technician will check the sign-in logs, reset your password, sign out all sessions and look for changes to your mailbox, such as new forwarding rules. If the sign-in was really you, for example while travelling, the technician will confirm and close the case.
