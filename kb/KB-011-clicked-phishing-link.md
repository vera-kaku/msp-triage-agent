---
id: KB-011
title: I clicked a link or entered my password on a suspicious page
queue: security
tags: [clicked, phishing, entered password, compromised, suspicious link, malware]
---
Act quickly. The first hour matters most.

1. Disconnect the laptop from Wi-Fi or unplug the network cable if you opened an attachment or downloaded a file.
2. Call the service desk immediately. Do not only send an email.
3. If you entered your password, a technician will reset it, sign you out of all sessions and check your mailbox for new forwarding rules.
4. Tell us what you clicked, at what time, and whether you entered a password or approved an MFA request.

Do not try to fix the laptop yourself and do not delete the email, because we may need it to check whether other staff received it. Reporting quickly is always the right thing to do, and you will not be in trouble for it.
