title: Teams microphone, speaker or camera not working
queue: service-desk
tags: [teams, microphone, camera, audio, headset, meeting, speaker]
---
Most Teams audio and camera problems come from Teams using the wrong device.

1. In a meeting, click More, then Settings, then Device settings.
2. Choose your headset or laptop under Speaker, Microphone and Camera.
3. Check that the camera is not covered by a privacy shutter and that no other app, such as Zoom, is using it.
4. Unplug the headset, plug it back in, and select it again.
5. Restart Teams. If that does not help, restart the laptop.

If the camera or microphone does not appear in the list at all after a restart, contact the service desk with the laptop and headset model. The driver may need to be reinstalled.
